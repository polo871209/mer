Mer privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Mer does not collect your data. Mer has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The developer of Mer cannot see your entries or anything else that you put in the app.

Your data stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud

Mer keeps your entries, categories, trips, budget, and preferences on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for Mer, iOS syncs this data between your devices through the private iCloud storage of your Apple Account. Only you can read that storage. Apple stores it under the Apple privacy policy.

Mer downloads exchange rates

At most once every 12 hours, Mer downloads a public list of exchange rates from exchange-api, through cdn.jsdelivr.net or currency-api.pages.dev. The request contains no information about you or your entries. As with every web request, these services receive your IP address.

Features that iOS runs for Mer

  • Face ID and your passcode: iOS checks them and tells Mer only whether the unlock worked. Mer never gets your face data or your passcode.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: Apple processes your voice request and gives Mer only the amount, category, currency, date, and note of the entry.
  • The Home Screen widget: the widget reads your category names and icons from your iPhone.
  • CSV import and export: Mer reads a file only when you pick it, and writes a file only when you export. The exported file goes only where you send it.

Delete your data

To delete an entry, swipe it in Mer. To delete everything, delete Mer from your iPhone, then delete Mer's data from your iCloud storage in the Settings app, under your Apple Account and iCloud.

Children

Mer collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page shows the new text and a new effective date.
